Common questions

Build a clear understanding of what a digital signature can—and cannot—prove.

Is a digital signature the same as an electronic signature? ＋

No. Electronic signature is a broad term for electronic ways of indicating agreement. A digital signature specifically uses cryptographic methods. Legal definitions vary by jurisdiction.

Does a valid signature mean a document is safe? ＋

Not necessarily. It helps check signer-key association and document integrity, but it does not prove that the document's claims are true or that the file contains no malware.

What happens if the document changes? ＋

The verification check will generally fail or report that the signature is invalid because the document no longer matches the signed content.
