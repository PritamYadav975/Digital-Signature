DIGITAL PUBLIC SERVICES
01 / E-GOVERNANCE

Public services, with fewer paper trails.

Government departments can use digital signatures to authenticate electronic records and approve transactions through secure workflows.

  • Online applications and declarations
  • Digitally signed certificates and official orders
  • Procurement documents and approvals
Example workflow

An officer signs an electronic approval. The recipient checks the certificate and signature before relying on the document.

⏱

Faster processing

Approvals can move online without printing, scanning, or physically shipping every document.

⌕

Easier verification

Recipients can check signature validity and whether signed content was altered.

♻

Less paperwork

Digital workflows can reduce paper handling and make records easier to manage.

ⓘ

Important: A digital signature does not automatically make every document legally valid. Requirements depend on local law, the type of record, certificate trust, and the organization's process.
